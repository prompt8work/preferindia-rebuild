Mechanical & Hydraulic Architecture

SYS-CODE: CU30-GA-001

The Prefer India CU-30-HD Coil Upender (Tilter) provides fail-safe 90° reorientation of high-mass hot-rolled or cold-rolled strip coils. Designed with heavy structural box-section beams welded under strict submerged arc processes, this system absorbs tremendous cantilevered inertia without deflection. High-torque twin hydraulic cylinders operate in synchronized tandem with counter-balance pilot valves to provide smooth, jerk-free tilting cycles even under full 30,000 kg asymmetric coil loads.

Zero-Shock Soft-Stop Hydraulic Cushioning

Integrated end-of-stroke deceleration cushions in dual Ø160mm hydraulic cylinders eliminate mechanical impact shock during 90° horizontal and vertical nesting phases.

Dual Counterbalance Fail-Safe Load Holding

Cylinder-port hard-mounted pilot-operated check valves guarantee absolute zero-creep lockup in the event of sudden hydraulic hose rupture or plant total blackouts.

Optical Proximity & Mill Interlocks

Industrial inductive IP67 rotary position encoders sync cycle limits with downstream automated transfer buggies, overhead C-hook cranes, and walking beam conveyors.
